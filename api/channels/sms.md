# SMS

::: warning Not available yet
KeenVector doesn't support SMS today. There's no SMS connection. `POST /v1/messages`
rejects `"channel": "sms"` with `400 invalid_request`. The **Send SMS** workflow step
appears in the builder but can't run, so a workflow that contains it can't be published.
:::

No date or feature set is promised. To hear when SMS becomes available, ask your
KeenVector account team or watch the [changelog](/api/changelog).

For channels you can use today, see [Connectors](/api/connectors/).
